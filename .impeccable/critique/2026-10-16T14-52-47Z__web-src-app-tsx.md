## Specificity verdict

the narrow heavy Archivo and the laminate ground refuse the dark broadcast grid and hold at both
widths. The lower half (weather, results tables) drifts back toward a generic analytics page.

## Findings, ranked

1. **P1: The verdict headlines a degenerate baseline.**
   - Where: `Verdict.tsx:36-66`, with data from `grid-order`. Screenshot `1-verdict-baseline-desktop.png`.
   - What's wrong: R17's favourite scores 42.2% at every stage (P1 0.422, every other driver
     0.018), so the page's biggest number never moves while the reader steps through the zones.
     The model name appears only in the next card's footer, and every Backtest says "No backtest
     published". A recruiter who looks closely loses trust here.
   - Fix (pipeline): register the `strength-pl` champion, which is an Ops item.
   - Fix (design): while `model.name` is a baseline, stamp BASELINE beside the score and name it in
     the sentence ("A grid-order baseline favours VER"). Consider showing the change since the
     previous stage.
2. **P1: The focus ring is invisible on "How it's built".**
   - Where: `styles.css:113` sets `--ring` ultramarine; the link at `Header.tsx:102` sits on the
     ultramarine band. Screenshot `3-band-link-focus-invisible.png`.
   - What's wrong: measured on Tab, the outline is 2px solid rgb(35,55,230) on #2337e6. That fails
     WCAG 2.4.7. The zone buttons already get a white ring, so only this link is affected.
   - Fix: give the link `focus-visible:outline-band-foreground`.
3. **P2: The uncalibrated wording is mostly right, with leaks.**
   - What's right: `probNoun()` drives the card subtitles, tooltips and the verdict caption ("win
     model score after Quali"), and every uncalibrated card shows the MODEL SCORES chip.
   - What leaks:
     - `DriverCard.tsx:128` "Likely finish" (it is the median position).
     - `Heatmap.tsx:16` GAIN_TIP: "most likely finish" and "the model expects".
     - The risk card's large "63% / 46% / 8%" stats (`Incidents.tsx:37-39`) carry no noun at all;
       only the chip says model scores.
   - Fix: "Median predicted finish"; put "model score" in the stat labels or captions while
     `calibrated` is false. Keeping "%" is defensible, since PRODUCT.md bans the noun, not the
     unit, as long as the label says model score.
4. **P2: The risk card breaks DESIGN.md.**
   - Where: `Incidents.tsx:14-16, 63-65`. Screenshot `2-risk-card-375.png`.
   - What's wrong:
     - The VSC bar is plain yellow, not the yellow-and-ink stripe.
     - The bars are `rounded-full` pills; radius should be 0.
     - The yellow fill on a pale track is about 1.5:1 non-text contrast.
     - The SC, VSC and red-flag figures appear twice, as stats and as bars.
   - Fix: square bars with a 1px ink border, a hatched VSC bar, and keep either the stats or the
     bars.
5. **P2: The stage and the clock can contradict each other.**
   - Where: Header. Screenshot `4-example-finished-vs-now.png`: the band says FINISHED while zone
     FP2 says NOW.
   - What's wrong: with F1's live-timing block, R18 sessions may fail to publish. The band would
     then say LIVE or FINISHED while the zones sit on an earlier stage, and the only hint is a
     stamp further down.
   - Fix: when the clock is past the published stage, say so in the status line under the band
     ("Race finished; latest prediction is from FP2"). Then it reads as a deliberate state, not
     a bug.
6. **P2: Qualifying predictions have no section.**

   - What's wrong: the example manifest has `qualifying: ready` and nothing renders, so the
